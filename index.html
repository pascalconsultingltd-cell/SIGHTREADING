THE OTHER WAY
  • No sharp, no flat: Do major or La minor.
  • Flat keys: Fa, and every key with ♭ in its name. All other keys use sharps.
  • Sharp key to signature: the last sharp is the note below the tonic. Say the order up to it.
  • Flat key to signature: say the order until one flat past the key name.
RELATIVE MAJOR AND MINOR
  • Major to relative minor: 3 frets down.
  • Minor to relative major: 3 frets up.

Same notes, same shape. Do major and La minor, Sol major and Mi minor.

WHERE THE NEXT TONIC FALLS
  • Octave: two strings up, two fingers up. Major starts on 2, octave on 4. Minor starts on 1, octave on 3.
  • Two octaves: high E string, same finger as on the low E string.
  • Tonic under the pinky on the low E string: next tonic is three strings up, under finger 1. Major scale: finger 2, hand one fret down.
TONIC, FIFTH, OCTAVE
  • Fourth: next string, same finger as the tonic.
  • Fifth: next string, two fingers up.
  • Octave: right above the fifth, same finger. Major: 4 on A, 4 on D. Minor: 3 on A, 3 on D.
THE START FINGER TELLS THE SCALE
  • Tonic on 1: the minor third is on the same string, finger 4.
  • Tonic on 2: the major third is on the next string, finger 1.
  • Tonic on 4: look at the next string. Finger 3 is major, finger 2 is minor.
THE FINGER BELOW THE TONIC
  • Major: the finger right below the tonic (1 then 2, or 3 then 4).
  • Natural minor: one finger gap (1 then 3).
  • Harmonic minor: natural minor with that note raised (2 then 3).
THE B STRING

The B string is tuned one fret lower than the pattern expects. Crossing from G to B, the hand moves up one fret. Every rule above gains one fret when it crosses the B string.

ONE SHAPE, THREE PLACES

Patterns 1, 3 and 5 are the same shape starting on the E, D and A strings. Only the B string changes it.

SCALES CONTAIN THE SMALLER ONES
  • Major arpeggio 1 uses the same fingers as major pattern 2, with notes left out.
  • Minor 7 arpeggio 1 is minor pentatonic 1 without the fourth.
  • Blues 1 is minor pentatonic 1 plus finger 2 on A and finger 4 on G.
