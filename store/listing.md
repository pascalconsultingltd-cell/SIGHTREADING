## Main store listing

**App name** (30 max)

    Sight-Reading Practice

**Short description** (80 max)

    Name the notes on the staff against the clock. Tap, sing or play your answer.

**Full description** (4000 max)

    Learn to read music faster. A note appears on the staff: name it before the clock runs out. How many can you get in 40 seconds?

    HOW IT WORKS
    • A note is shown on the staff.
    • Tap its name on the keyboard, or turn on the microphone and sing it or play it on your instrument.
    • Right answer: next note. Wrong answer: it is kept for review.
    • At the end, look at each mistake with the right answer next to it.

    MAKE IT AS HARD AS YOU LIKE
    • Treble clef, or treble and bass clef together
    • Sharps and flats, on or off
    • Normal range (notes on the staff) or extended range (ledger lines)
    • Note sound on or off
    • Best score kept for each range

    FOR ANY INSTRUMENT
    Keys show both solfège (Do Re Mi) and letter names (C D E). With the microphone on, the app listens for the note in any octave, so it works with voice, guitar, piano, violin, flute and more.

    SIMPLE AND PRIVATE
    • No account, no adverts, no tracking
    • Works offline
    • Microphone sound is analysed on your device and never recorded or sent

    A few minutes a day is enough to stop counting lines and start reading.
