Privacy policy

Sight-Reading Practice · last updated 11 October 2026

Sight-Reading Practice is a game for learning to read music notes. This policy covers the Android app and the website at pascalconsultingltd-cell.github.io/SIGHTREADING.

Summary

The app collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Microphone

The microphone is off by default. If you turn on "Sing or play the note", the app asks for permission and listens only while a game is running, to work out which note you sing or play. The sound is analysed on your device as it comes in. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. You can turn the microphone off in the app, or withdraw the permission in your device settings, at any time.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (sound, microphone, accidentals, clefs, note range) and your best scores are saved in the app's local storage on your device, so they are still there next time. They never leave the device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes them.

Hosting

The app's files are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address when the files are downloaded. The developer has no access to these logs. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/pascalconsultingltd-cell/SIGHTREADING/issues.
